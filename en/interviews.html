This page lists the 1:1 interviews conducted with founders, builders and actors of the XRPL ecosystem. Each interview produces a set of opportunity propositions which are then integrated into /opportunities with the source-tag INT-{name}.

Why: internal analyses (PWGs + ATKA + ecosystem-map + YC RFS) capture structural and protocol-level frictions well, but miss the ground-truth qualitative signals — the voice of the builder who touches the code, the founder who pivots, the dev who struggles. Interviews fill this gap.

Strategic brief — Protocol state

Live 2025: MPT (Oct), Credentials, AMM Clawback, dNFT (June), Single Asset Vault (XLS-65)
Inbound 2026: Lending Protocol (XLS-66 in validator voting), Permissioned DEX

Strategic brief — Macro context

XRPL #2 globally in 30-day RWA growth at 15.37% (behind Arbitrum only, ahead of Solana/Polygon/Avalanche). Volume is concentrated: very few holders, dominated by stablecoins + a handful of institutional instruments.

"RFS + live traction" rather than Solana-style open-theme. XRPL Commons publishes a short-list of wanted products (Y Combinator RFS model) → clear brief, objective judging, output that fills real gaps.

Live traction gate (deployed mainnet + real on-chain activity by day 30) = filters out polished demos, rewards those who understand distribution. ✅ Already applied in the May 12 prizes FIX (30k USD volume-gated pool).

Key takeaway

Florence brings a multi-layer strategic brief: 5 concrete new proposals with quantified volume potential + macro reading of XRPL's position (#2 RWA growth) + directly actionable buildathon format recommendation. Her 5 opps all target the consumer/mid-market gap on top of institutional primitives already live (MPT, Credentials, AMM, dNFT, XLS-65).

📡 External signal
ContextPublic LinkedIn post by Kayla Phillips (crypto/VC analyst) recapping her Consensus 2026 takeaways. Not a 1:1 interview — public intel digested and tagged for our strategy. LinkedIn source →
FormatPublic LinkedIn post (Consensus 2026 takeaways) · macro reading, no direct proposals
⚠️ XRPL-bearish signal · Canton dominates institutional mindshare

Kayla: "In terms of chains, Canton stole the show. Lots of chatter about partnering and building on Canton; little mention of any other chains." Zero mention of XRPL at Consensus 2026. Canton is capturing the tradfi/institutional RWA mindshare. Buildathon implication: avoid the frontal fight on pure institutional RWA (Canton wins) and focus on the 3 sweet spots where XRPL has an edge: (1) consumer/retail (payments, remittance, gaming, dNFT, DeFi UX) · (2) mid-market/SMB (B2B treasury, accounting, merchant tooling) · (3) AI agents / agentic commerce (race still open). Consistent with Florence's brief (#04).

✓ Market validations · Mastercard B2B + Visa agentic commerce

Kayla: "Mastercard is very focused on B2B payments expansion while Visa aims to dominate agentic commerce." Two major players polarize the 2026 market and directly validate our registry: Mastercard B2B reinforces OPP-018 (B2B Stablecoin FX desk — Florence) and OPP-031 (Wise-on-XRPL). Visa agentic commerce reinforces OPP-052 (AgentPay x402+XRPL) and OPP-063 (AI agent credit card RLUSD).

✓ Thesis validation · Capital migrating to Crypto + AI

Kayla: "Most former crypto funds are now doing crypto & AI" + "All people wanted to talk about is AI". Confirms the relevance of the ai-agents theme in the registry (OPP-052..063, 12 opps). Tradfi VC capital is migrating to this thesis — it's where the money is flowing in 2026-2027.

General sentiment note

Consensus 2026 sentiment = weak / bearish despite the announcements (Clarity Act, Haun/a16z mega funds, Bullish + Equiniti $4.2B, Kraken + Reap $600M). The buildathon ships into an uncertain market — live traction quality (Section 05 gate) will be all the more discriminating.
