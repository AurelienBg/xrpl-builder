This page lists the 1:1 interviews conducted with founders, builders and actors of the XRPL ecosystem. Each interview produces a set of opportunity propositions which are then integrated into /opportunities with the source-tag INT-{name}.

Why: internal analyses (PWGs + ATKA + ecosystem-map + YC RFS) capture structural and protocol-level frictions well, but miss the ground-truth qualitative signals — the voice of the builder who touches the code, the founder who pivots, the dev who struggles. Interviews fill this gap.
