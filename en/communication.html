Section 00

Strategic synthesis — Buildathon positioning

Recommendation

Hybrid B + C · annual flagship + continuous waves

A 30-day flagship Buildathon (Colosseum model) + 2-3 continuous 14-day waves per year (Akindo model)

The 3 models are not mutually exclusive. The market shows that ecosystems producing serious founders combine a flagship event (strong signal, media coverage, deadline that forces delivery) + an ongoing program (continuous activation, permanent onboarding, milestone-based iteration).

  • Pros: year-round ecosystem activity · robust VC pipeline · deadline-driven + iteration-driven · continuous onboarding
  • Cons: 2 systems to operate (an event + a platform) · requires on-chain infrastructure for waves · more organizational overhead
XRPL Commons journey · the Buildathon in the chain

the Buildathon fits into a broader journey within the XRPL Commons ecosystem. It is the "test on mainnet with real users" link in the chain:

📚 Academy  →  🛠 Buildathon  →  🐠 Aquarium  →  🚀 Startup Studio (coming soon)
  • The Academy prepares upstream (workshops, mentoring, educational content). The entrepreneur track will bundle the existing apps — Pitch/Canvas, Regulation, Gamification, Tokenlab — into a unified experience. The content produced during the Buildathon (best-practices office hours, recordings) feeds this track afterward.
  • The Buildathon tests builders on mainnet over 30 days, with real users and measurable on-chain activity (Section 05 gate). Target: projects reaching a few hundred to ~1000 users.
  • The Aquarium incubates top projects post-buildathon (the 1st major prize wins a direct entry — see Section 05).
  • The Startup Studio (coming soon) will support the most mature startups beyond.

Note: the idea of a Perpetual Hackathon (Akindo/WaveHack-style, cf. Model C) stays on the table as a distinct project to explore after the 2026 Buildathon — not for this edition.

Section 01

Name — what do we call this thing?

Proposal

Generic, sober, SEO-friendly name. The subtitle "XRPL Commons · 30 days" carries context and duration.

We keep the word everyone understands instantly, with no marketing overhead. The program leans on its mechanics (30 days, 100k XRP hybrid pool, milestone-grants + small prizes) rather than an invented brand. Section 02

Format & cadence — how long, what rhythm?

Proposal

30 days · remote-first + optional IRL Paris 1d/week

Day 0 kickoff → 28 days build → Day 29 freeze → Day 30 Demo Day. Remote-first · 1 optional IRL day/week at the Commons House Paris (cf. Section 09).

Compact 30-day sprint, fully online. No travel, no in-person logistics — every team competes on equal footing globally. Long enough to ship a credible MVP + working demo (4 weeks of build), short enough to maintain urgency. Launch aligned with the Research Summit (Day 0). Annual format first — possible extension to continuous mode (3 cohorts/year) in a future edition (distinct project, not for 2026).

  • Day 0: Kickoff online, presentation and Q&A session. → Enrollment open Day 0 → Day 14 (cutoff). Teams can join mid-way but all must hit the Day 30 gate.
  • Day 1 → Day 28: Build phase — 2 office-hour sessions/week (30min each) + 1 optional IRL day/week at the Commons House (cf. Section 09)
  • Day 29: Pitch deck freeze + Demo freeze — code, deck, video demo all locked
  • Day 30: Demo Day online (livestream) — investor-oriented (not a "fun demo": fund-raising-ready pitch). Mandatory gate: project deployed on XRPL mainnet with measurable on-chain activity via SourceTag (volume, transactions, addresses). Jury review · prizes announcement.
🛠 Platform & tools — to be decided by the team

Submission · jury review · criteria tracking · prizes announcement: HACKATHON platform considered as the operational backbone.
Participant tracking · communications · community: possible complementary use of the PULSE app. Final platform decision to be arbitrated internally — depends on required features and possible integration with SourceTag for on-chain tracking.

Section 04

Challenge register — what can teams build?

Proposal

Registry /opportunities — 44 APP opportunities, 5 themes

The Buildathon challenge register = the APP subset of the unified registry /opportunities (single source of truth · convergence PWG + ATKA + ECO + YC + interviews). The 30 TOOL opps (libraries, primitives, SDKs) are out of scope for the Buildathon — intended for existing teams / core contributors. Community-useful TOOL projects may however be eligible for the GLOW grant (XRPL Commons).
🟢 Key message to builders

The OPPs in the registry are identified opportunities — ideas where we believe a potential need exists (converging signals from PWG + ATKA + ecosystem + YC + founder interviews). They are in no way a restrictive framework. All XRPL projects are allowed and welcome — whether they map to an OPP, combine several, or propose something completely different. The registry is a starting point for builders looking for inspiration, not a mandatory checklist.

→ If your project comes from a previously started project, that's totally OK. No obligation to start from scratch on Day 0 — an existing project that ships a new mainnet milestone during the 30-day Buildathon is just as eligible. The only criterion that matters is the Section 05 gate (mainnet deployment + measurable on-chain activity by day 30). Fairness: for existing projects, metrics (on-chain volume, # users) are counted from program launch — we measure the delta between Day 0 and Day 30, so new projects aren't penalized against those already holding traction.

→ Entrepreneur tools: XRPL Commons provides a suite of tools (Academy entrepreneur track: Pitch/Canvas, Regulation, Gamification, Tokenlab) to help teams consolidate and improve their project during and after the Buildathon.

→ Realistic ambition: we target projects reaching a few hundred real users on mainnet by end of the 30 days, up to ~1000 users for the most ambitious. no need to aim for 10k+ users to win.

🏷 Enrollment · day-0 requirements
  • Public code repo (or justified if proprietary) — declared at enrollment
  • Identified contributor list — light KYC required for prize payout
  • SourceTag active day 1 — integrated in the repo from kickoff, auto on-chain monitoring via the HACK platform
  • Flexible enrollment — open Day 0 → Day 14 (cutoff). Teams can join mid-way but the Day 30 gate stays fixed (≥16 days build minimum).
  • Mandatory weekly video — 60-120s progress update each week (cf. Section 09 Team Support). For visibility AND progress monitoring.

SourceTag automatically generates a project ID card (repo + contributors + tag) feeding the leaderboard and tracking through Day 30.

⚠️ Removal mechanism (jury-led, not weekly)

The jury can remove a project from leaderboard and prize pool on signal of: (1) scam / bidon project detected by mentors or community · (2) 2 missed weekly videos = automatic flag → jury review · (3) no repo activity (commits) for 10+ days. No automatic weekly elimination — jury decision communicated to team with 48h right of reply.

⚠️ Out-of-scope · infra blockers to drive in parallel

4 infra gaps identified (Kevin interview) are out of Buildathon scope as not buildable in 30 days, but must be driven by Foundation / Ripple in parallel to unblock the ecosystem:

  • Wallet activation cost — Foundation fund to cover activation per project
  • Stablecoin on-ramps (USDC/RLUSD) — Moonpay/Transak partnerships to drive
  • Virtual IBAN accounts — Meld/Bridge alignment to reduce onboarding fees (4.5% → 0.75%)
  • Native XRPL card issuance API — competitive gap vs Solana, M&A or Stripe-like partnership

Teams choose a challenge from the 44 APP opportunities in the registry (structure validated by Paris Blockchain Week working groups + convergent ecosystem signals), spread across 5 themes (builder-primitives is now 100% TOOL → out of scope for the buildathon): Wallets (7) · B2B (8) · RWAT (7) · DeFi (13) · AI Agents (9). Prize bracket mapped on the 3 registry tiers:

  • Tier HIGH (×7 APP challenges) → Grand Prix Bracket. The highest-impact user-facing opps (see /en/opportunities filtered by tier HIGH).
  • Tier MEDIUM (×23 APP challenges) → Major Prize. User-facing opps with strong demand (community survey · regulatory window · top-of-mind founders).
  • Tier LOW (×11 APP challenges) → Standard Prize. Lower-priority user-facing opps (specific verticals, novelties).
  • Open category: teams can propose an off-registry APP challenge if justified; jury decides eligibility. If retained and impactful, the opportunity is added to the registry.

Section 05

Prizes — what do winners get?

Proposal

Pool flex 50–100k XRP · Small prizes + top 6 milestone-grants + bonus #1 + Aquarium

2026 annual budget — flexible envelope 50–100k XRP (target 50k, up to 100k with sponsors). Structure: 100 × 300 XRP small prizes (30k) + top 6 × 3k XRP milestone-grants (18k) + bonus #1 · +2k XRP + Aquarium access for 1st place.
Eligibility gate — mandatory for any prize
  • Project deployed on XRPL mainnet by day 30
  • Measurable on-chain activity by day 30 via SourceTag: volume · transactions · unique addresses

Filters out polished demos, rewards real traction (mainnet + users + volume). Inspired by the "RFS + live traction" model (Florence interview).

🥉 Top 6 milestone-grants · 3k XRP × 6 projects

6 milestone-grants of 3k XRP each, awarded to the top 6 projects based on scoring (cf. Section 07). Paid in 2 short tranches to reward both the build effort and post-launch traction:

Milestone When Amount
Upfront At results announcement (Day 30) 2,000 XRP
Milestone M+3 3 months post-buildathon (Oct 2026) 1,000 XRP
Total / project 3,000 XRP

Paid in 2 tranches: 2k XRP on Day 30 (to reward the build effort) + 1k XRP at M+3 on a co-built traction criterion. Simple, short, no long-term post-Day 30 constraint.

🏆 Bonus #1 · +2k XRP for the n°1 project

Cherry on top: the project that finishes 1st in scoring receives, in addition to its 3k XRP milestone-grant, a bonus 2k XRP paid on Day 30. That's 5k XRP cash total for #1 (2k upfront + 2k bonus + 1k M+3).

🐠 Aquarium Access · for the n°1 project

Top 1 wins, in addition to the cash grants, a direct entry to the Aquarium — XRPL Commons incubation program, now positioned as an accelerator (1:1 mentorship, tech/business coaching, investor access). Stepping stone to Startup Studio (coming soon).

🎟 Small prizes · 300 XRP × up to 100 projects

No hard cap on the number. Every project that passes the live-traction gate receives a small prize of 500 XRP. Dedicated pool of 50k XRP — up to 100 qualifying projects. If more projects qualify, jury arbitrates on secondary engagement (on-chain volume, user count, retention).

Total budget ~50k XRP (flex envelope 50–100k) 100 × 300 XRP small prizes (30k) + 6 × 3k XRP milestone-grants (18k) + bonus #1 +2k XRP + Aquarium access (perk). 2026 annual pool — envelope extensible up to 100k XRP with sponsor co-funding. Multi-edition possible if renewed next year.
Section 06

Jury composition — who decides?

Proposal

XRPL Commons team · composition to be finalized

Single-organization jury — clean ownership, fast decisions. Names finalized internally.

The XRPL Commons team runs the jury alone. Knows the builders, owns the roadmap, the program quality and the Section 07 criteria evaluation. No coordination overhead, no scheduling nightmare, clear accountability if a result is contested. Exact composition (titular jurors + alternates) decided internally, no public announcement of names.

  • Pros: simple, fast, clear ownership, no scheduling friction
  • Cons: no external advocacy/accountability balance — risk of being seen as too internal (mitigated by the transparency of Section 07 criteria and the public M+3 milestone validation)
Section 07

Judging criteria — how is "winning" measured?

Proposal

2-step judging — Binary gate, then bracket-specific scoring

Criteria apply differently depending on the prize bracket (small prizes vs major milestone-grants). All published to candidates upfront.

⛔ Step 1 — Live-traction gate (binary · mandatory for any prize)

All-or-nothing criteria by Day 30. Without these 2 conditions, no prize is awarded (cf. Section 05):

  • Mainnet deployment — project deployed and accessible on XRPL mainnet, source code published (or justified if proprietary)
  • Measurable on-chain activity via SourceTag — at least 1 real transaction attributable to the project via SourceTag, verifiable by the jury on XRPL explorer

🎟 Step 2a — Small prizes 300 XRP (simple gate-based scoring)

Any project that passes the gate automatically receives the 300 XRP small prize — no secondary scoring (unless the pool saturates). If more than 100 projects qualify, jury arbitrates on engagement metrics (descending order):

  1. Cumulative on-chain volume (USD-equivalent) over the 30 days
  2. Unique addresses having interacted with the project
  3. Transaction count attributable

🥉 Step 2b — Top 6 milestone-grants 3k XRP (multi-criteria scoring)

6 milestone-grants of 3k XRP awarded based on weighted scoring among projects that passed the gate. The n°1 project also receives a +2k XRP bonus and the Aquarium access. Total scoring = 100%.

Criterion What it measures Weight
🚀 On-chain traction Cumulative volume (USD-eq) · unique addresses · transaction count · retention (return-users) — objective metrics via SourceTag 50%
🔧 Build Quality Smooth UX/UI, feature completeness, docs, accessibility (mobile/desktop), overall polish + code quality, security, architecture, audit-readiness (smart contracts if applicable), open-source preferred 30%
📣 Pitch & narrative Video demo clarity (60-120s), one-pager deck, why-now, story-telling, fund-raising-ready pitch ability (investor-oriented Demo Day) 20%
Total 100%

🏆 Bonus #1 + Aquarium Access · for the n°1 project

The project ranked #1 (highest weighted score across the 5 criteria) wins, in addition to the milestone-grant, the direct Aquarium entry (cf. Section 05).

🔄 Milestone M+3 post-Day 30 (unlock criterion for the last 1k XRP payment)

The top 6 milestone-grants are paid in 2 tranches: 2k XRP upfront (Day 30) + 1k XRP at M+3 months under condition. Criterion co-built with each team in the 2 weeks post-Day 30 — aligned with their own roadmap. Standard frame:

  • M+3 (3 months) — typically: traction doubled (users / volume), N+1 feature roadmap shipped, first partnership / integration signed, legal structure finalized

Milestones are objective measurable, not vague. XRPL Commons validates completion before payment unlock.

Section 08

Team composition — size & profile?

Proposal

Solo or team — no minimum, no maximum, no profile requirements

Maximum accessibility — anyone can apply, alone or with any cofounders. No prior profile required.
  • No minimum — solo applicants are welcome and eligible for all prize tiers (small prizes + major milestone-grants + Aquarium access)
  • No maximum — teams of any size can apply
  • No profile required — no minimum XRPL experience, no minimum crypto experience, no seniority required. If you can build, you're eligible.

  • Aquarium / Hackathon alumni are eligible for all prizes without distinction (the former "From the community" prize was removed)
Section 09 · NEW

Team Support — full support for teams during the build

Proposal

5 team-support levers · weekly cadence + office hours + mentorship + peer learning + communication / GTM

During the 4-week build phase (Day 1 → Day 28, 17 Jun → 14 Jul)

🚢 Weekly Ship Cadence (Guillaume)

  • Mandatory weekly video: (1) short video (60-120s) of progress · (2) single-slide deck · (3) user feedback on socials (X post with connected wallet + XRP profile for traceability). 2 missed weeks = jury review flag (cf. Section 04 removal mechanism).
  • XRPL Commons amplifies: weekly push of the top 5 or 10 on its channels + single public link to view all submissions
  • Merch reward for the best comments / feedback received by each team (qualified-feedback verification: connected wallet, active XRP profile, no bots)

🎤 Fixed Office Hours + IRL

  • 🏠 Build at the Commons House — 1 day per week, come build your project at our office. IRL coworking open to Paris-based teams (or in transit), in-person access to XRPL Commons mentors, lunch on site. Fixed day TBD (likely Tuesday or Thursday, aligned with one of the office hours).
  • Tuesday morning · 10:00–10:30 CET — Tech Office Hour (XRPL devrel + Ripple infra). Topics: XLS-66 / XLS-65 / XLS-70 specs, code review, integration debugging. Live group call (Zoom), recorded.
  • Thursday afternoon · 16:00–16:30 CET — Product / GTM Office Hour (XRPL Commons + Aquarium alum). Topics: positioning, pitch shaping, distribution strategy, RLUSD/CEX integrations. Live group call, recorded.
  • Option: 30min best-practice segment integrated in each session — captures fed back into the Academy (entrepreneur track).
  • On-demand AMA — async via dedicated Discord channel. ≤24h response from a rotating mentor pool of ~10 people.

👨‍🏫 1:1 Mentorship + community founder intros (Guillaume)

  • Dedicated Mentor sessions (in addition to collective office hours) — each team matched with a referent mentor for 30min/week. Bookable via shared Calendly. Covers product strategy, IP/legal, fundraising prep.
  • Founder intros from the XRPL Commons community — each team can request 2-3 qualified intros (Aquarium alumni, EU/SEA ecosystem, RLUSD partners). Goal: direct dealflow, not surface networking.
  • Recorded archive of all collective sessions, accessible to all teams + future reference.

🤝 Peer learning · horizontal learning

  • Cross-team Discord channel — pattern sharing, collective debug, peer code reviews. Light moderation by XRPL Commons.
  • Open team-to-team sessions — 1 slot/week where one team presents their current blocker and the cohort helps. No formal mentor, pure horizontal.
  • Insight: the real value of an accelerator lies in peer learning more than in formal workshops — teams that solve together learn faster than those who listen to a mentor.
  • Post-Buildathon continuity: news.xrpl-commons.org remains the community exchange space for members after Day 30 — Buildathon builders continue discussions and progress sharing there.

📣 Communication & GTM · public push of ready projects

  • Conditional communication: public push of a project only when it is ready for users (solid build + working onboarding). No over-promo of bugs or broken demos — each XRPL Commons amplification must serve the end user, not just the story.
  • "King-making" strategy (inspired by Solana model): XRPL Commons + team + advocates (David, Florence, Guillaume) personally engage mature projects on socials — retweets, threads, qualified intros. Like Anatoly Yakovenko with Solana builders.
  • GTM focus: the #1 difficulty for XRPL devs = distribution & monetization. Realistic mid-term target for a 2-person team = ~10k€/month recurring revenue. Team Support orients projects toward this KPI from Day 1 (not only on-chain volume traction).
  • Open offices 2-3 days at start or end of Buildathon — possibility of IRL Commons House events for kick-off or pre-Demo Day (to arbitrate based on candidate flow).

Why this design: creates a public weekly heartbeat (teams ship and the market sees it), paired with deep support (1:1 mentor + IRL Commons House). The merch + XRPL Commons amplification gamify user engagement WITHOUT relying solely on on-chain volume (Section 05 gate) — filtering out bots without killing early-stage projects that don't yet have volume.

Section 12

🎯 Program variant — hybrid milestone-grants

Recommendation

Variant 1 · Buildathon · Hybrid milestone-grants + XRPL Commons journey

Simple format, flex 50–100k XRP annual pool, milestone-based, integrated in the Academy → Aquarium → Startup Studio journey

Format:

  • 30-day Buildathon · remote-first + optional IRL Paris 1d/week · open (no curated selection) · target a few hundred → ~1000 users on mainnet per project · mandatory live-traction gate (mainnet deployed + measurable on-chain activity via SourceTag by day 30)
  • Flex pool 50–100k XRP · hybrid structure (cf. Section 05):
    • Up to 100 small prizes × 300 XRP = 30k XRP
    • Top 6 × 3k XRP milestone-grants (2k upfront + 1k M+3) = 18k XRP
    • Bonus #1 · +2k XRP paid Day 30 = 2k XRP
    • Aquarium access (perk) for the n°1 project
  • Weekly "ship + visibility" cadence · weekly delivery (video + deck + X feedback) · Commons amplification top 5-10 · merch reward for best feedback · 1 IRL day/week at the Commons House (cf. Section 09)
  • XRPL Commons journey integration · content produced (workshops, mentoring, office hours) feeds the Academy · top 1 major prize enters the Aquarium directly · continuation to Startup Studio (coming soon)

Pros: simple to operate (1 FTE is enough) · pool focused on real traction (not demo) · milestone-grants engage winners long-term · Aquarium access = real reward beyond cash · format renewable (multi-edition possible if successful).

Cons: fewer flagship moments than Colosseum-style · no big "$300k pool" headline · depends on candidate flow (open vs curated). Mitigated by: Buildathon marketing, Commons weekly amplification, upstream Academy that prepares the funnel.

Estimated cost: ~$70-150k for the 1st edition (flex pool 50–100k XRP + ops/tooling/marketing). Possible multi-edition next year if renewed. Very low overhead vs Variants 2/3.

🎯 2027+ ambition (out of 2026 scope)

$1M-style pool (Avalanche Build Games, Solana Colosseum) = credible ambition for 2027+ if renewal and sponsor co-financing. For 2026 (1st edition), the 50-100k XRP flex pool is validated as the right "prove the format then scale" ratio. To re-arbitrate post-Buildathon 2026 based on results.
