The thesis in one sentence

XRPL becomes the L1 of Institutional & Compliant DeFi — RLUSD as settlement stablecoin, XLS-66/65 as lending/vault rails, XLS-70/80 as Identity infra, EVM Sidechain as the cross-chain innovation zone. The bottleneck is not the protocol — it's distribution (CeDeFi via CEX) and integration plumbing (data, accounting, KYC).

Meta-finding · do not forget

The XRPL ecosystem suffers from an awareness gap, not a protocol gap. 76% of items identified as "missing" by the Working Groups are already addressed by existing primitives.

Direct consequence: M-22 Capabilities Registry + M-21 Pre-Deployed Testnet alone have more impact than the majority of products on this list. A Buildathon must allocate a meaningful share to these structural multipliers.
