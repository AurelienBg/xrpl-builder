Section 06 · The unified strategic frame

Final strategic frame for XRPL Builders

Distillation of both analyses: what a founder needs to know before starting.

The thesis in one sentence

XRPL becomes the L1 of Institutional & Compliant DeFi — RLUSD as settlement stablecoin, XLS-66/65 as lending/vault rails, XLS-70/80 as Identity infra, EVM Sidechain as the cross-chain innovation zone (Morpho/Babylon/Pendle composables). The bottleneck is not the protocol, it's distribution (CeDeFi via CEX) and integration plumbing (data, accounting, KYC).

For a founder — top 5 bets

  • M-24 CeDeFi Yield SDK — distribution play #1, 60%+ XRP on CEXes
  • M-09 Vault Wrapper — timing arbitrage on XLS-65, institutional capital ready
  • M-14 Yield-Bearing Stablecoin — top community demand × USDe did $5B in < 1 year
  • M-17 Restaking for XRP — Babylon did $6B on BTC, XRP market $110B
  • M-26 AgentPay (x402) — AI agents narrative is trendy, XRPL perfectly positioned

For XRPL Commons — top 5 ecosystem priorities

  • M-21 Pre-Deployed Testnet — multiplier across everything else
  • M-22 Capabilities Registry — solves the awareness gap directly
  • M-25 P-256 / secp256r1 — highest-leverage XLS (Apple/Android/HSM)
  • M-05 Institutional Data Adapter — unblocks every future institutional integration
  • M-07 Multi-Flow ACL — unblocks institutional compliance flows

Persistent tensions

  • T4 RLUSD-first vs multi-stablecoin — risk profile choice vs Ripple dependency
  • Speed-to-market via fork (Morpho/Babylon) vs originality XRPL-native (Risk-Signal MPT) — each founder picks their angle
  • Pure DeFi vs service company — revenue model × execution capability

Meta-finding (do not forget)

  • The ecosystem suffers from an awareness gap, not a protocol gap — 76% of "missing" items identified by WGs are already addressed by existing primitives.
  • Direct consequence: a capabilities registry (M-22) + pre-deployed sandbox (M-21) alone have more impact than the majority of products on the list.
  • A Buildathon must therefore allocate a non-negligible fraction of its resources to these two structural multipliers.
