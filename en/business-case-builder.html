Assumptions · methodology

Model: monthly projection over 24 months. Cash = initial capex − cumulative opex + cumulative revenue. Monthly compound growth on active users, dampened by churn. Revenue = ARPU × active users. Opex includes salaries (which may grow with scale) + infra + marketing + compliance + marginal XRPL fees (negligible).

Model limits:

  • No seasonality or non-linear growth steps (PR launches, viral loops…)
  • CAC and ARPU assumed constant over 24 months — in reality both rise
  • No intermediate fundraising rounds factored in — the capital shown is the amount needed in a self-funded or single pre-seed scenario
  • LTV computed as ARPU / churn · approximation classique mais conservative

Tweak the assumptions in the left panel to test different scenarios. Presets start from data drawn from the Builder Atlas — they are calibrated but should be challenged.
