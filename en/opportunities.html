This page is the unified registry of opportunities to build on XRPL — single source of truth for the buildathon. It replaces /master-gaps and /gaps (kept accessible temporarily with redirect banner).

Format: 6 themes × 2 types (APP/TOOL), each opportunity tagged with its original sources (PWG, ATKA, ECO, YC, INT-djason/xavier/florian/florence/guillaume). Click a row for details. Filters (Type · Tier · Chain · Source) are multi-select: OR within an axis, AND across axes.

🟢 Key message

The OPPs listed here are identified opportunities — ideas where we believe a potential need exists (converging signals from PWG + ATKA + ecosystem + YC + founder interviews). They are in no way a restrictive framework. All XRPL projects are allowed and welcome — whether they map to an OPP, combine several, or propose something completely different. The registry is a starting point for builders looking for inspiration, not a mandatory checklist.
