AI × Crypto Gap 07 ★

x402 XRPL facilitator

Coinbase shipped it on Base with USDC. On XRPL: zero implementations.

GREENFIELD ★★★★ TICKET MID READY NOW

x402 is the HTTP 402 "Payment Required" standard revived by Coinbase to let AI agents pay for APIs/services in crypto without login or credit card. Live on Base/USDC. XRPL has no facilitator, even though its specs are ideal for this use case: ~0.00001 XRP per tx, 3–5s finality, native payments. The XRPL facilitator would be an HTTP middleware that validates the Payment on-chain and unlocks the API. Market exploding with OpenAI Operator, Claude Computer Use, Vercel AI agents.

Market Paid APIs + AI agents · agent-to-API TAM growing exponentially
Why now x402 standard published · first wave of agents in production · XRPL absent from the landscape

AI-native services · YC RFS lens

8 services · 60–90% gross margin economics

YC's RFS "AI-native service companies" flips the question. Instead of asking "what tool is missing on XRPL?", we ask "what service is still done by humans that an AI agent could do better?". Instead of selling the tool, you become the service.

Economics change everything: classic SaaS hits ~75% gross margin with $50–500/month ARPU. AI-native services = ~70–90% margin with $5–30k/month ARPU. 10–100× the revenue per account at near-equivalent margins.

XRPL is exceptionally well-positioned: native multisig + RegularKey (autonomous AI agent with human guardrails, no smart contract needed), XLS-70 DID + Credentials (carry your license on your agent), ~zero fees (every agent action logged on-chain for audit).

The 8 ideas below are scored on 4 service-specific axes: market maturity, size (revenue TAM), required regulation, and target gross margin. Each card ends with a Moat line coding 1-2 letters for defensibility (T tech AI · R regulatory · N network effect · C capital · B brand).
