CONTEXT This page consolidates the first reading of the 6 founding documents of the Buildathon: the 4 transcripts of the XRPL Zone Paris Product Working Groups (RWA, Stablecoins, Lending, Identity & Credentials) held in April 2026, the PWG 4 summary PDF, and the XRPL DeFi Report by Atka (89 slides, January 2026).

METHOD Three-pass reading: (1) cross-cutting themes that recur in 3+ documents; (2) per-PWG findings; (3) strategic tensions to arbitrate. This analysis is then confronted with Analysis 2 (external challenger) to produce the Synthesis.

USAGE This analysis feeds the Buildathon challenge register. The 5 strategic tensions formalized here are the choices left to the founder — not arbitrated by default.

The thesis in one sentence

XRPL is shifting from a payment L1 to an Institutional DeFi L1 — but this shift is hindered by four structural frictions that recur across all documents.

Strategic conclusion: the next wave of XRPL builders will need to build not "pure DeFi" products, but institutional CeDeFi products with native compliance (DID/Credentials), that solve liquidity fragmentation (cross-XRPL/EVM) and treat RLUSD as default settlement infrastructure.

Section 01 · The 4 structural frictions

Four structural frictions

Recur across 3+ of the 6 documents. A significant portion of the Buildathon should be dedicated to addressing them.

01

Fragmented liquidity XRPL/EVM

Each PWG (Lending, RWA, Stablecoins) ends up hitting the question "where is liquidity, how do we make it flow" between native XRPL and the EVM Sidechain.

02

Default compliance absent

XLS-70 (DID) and Credentials exist but real-flow usage (reusable KYC, transferable attestations, transitive accreditation) is not wired up. No product in production.

03

Stablecoin fragmentation

RLUSD is growing fast (> $150M) but USDC/USDT are missing. B2B/RWA use cases require a stable, MiCA-compatible stablecoin map.

04

Off-chain distribution

60% of circulating XRP is on CEXes or held by Ripple Foundation. XRPL DeFi will only reach these holders through the CEXes — CeDeFi is mandatory, not optional.

Section 02 · 5 cross-cutting themes

Cross-cutting themes (4+ docs)

Five patterns recurring across RWA, Stablecoins, Lending, Identity and the Atka report.

B.1The "compliance gap" is the #1 blocker for institutional adoption

PWG 4: KYC duplicated N times by each issuer. PWG 2 RWA: token-level whitelist/permissioning (MPT XLS-33d enables it, lightly adopted). PWG 1 Lending: institutional pools = onchain accreditation. PWG 3 Stablecoins: MiCA + Travel Rule. Pattern: "Identity & Credentials" is not a vertical — it's the horizontal infra that unblocks the other 3.

B.2XRPL native vs EVM Sidechain: the civil war

Atka devotes 30+ slides to the EVM Sidechain as growth vector #1 (Restaking, Morpho, Pendle, Babylon). PWG 1 Lending speaks almost exclusively about native (XLS-66). PWG 2 RWA mixes both. No document settles the question. Opportunity: "chain-agnostic XRPL" products that expose ONE interface and route to native OR EVM depending on the use case.

B.3RLUSD as "USDC-killer institutional"

Atka: RLUSD = $150M+ issued in less than 12 months, growth > initial USDe. PWG Stablecoins: MiCA-ready, NY-DFS regulated. PWG RWA: Ondo + OpenEden $2bn TVL combined, fueled in large part by RLUSD as settlement pair. PWG Lending: RLUSD = premium collateral. Any serious XRPL B2B product must have RLUSD at its core.

B.4The "first user" problem (chicken-and-egg)

Atka §64-66: Avalanche Rush ($180M), Polygon BD push, Base Build, Morpho×Base partnership. PWGs 1 and 2: not enough serious builders, not enough initial liquidity. Repeated solution: $XRP incentives + partnerships with cross-chain leaders (Morpho, Babylon, Pendle, Securitize).

B.5The "service company" vs "tooling" thesis

PWG 2 RWA and PWG 4 Identity insist: RWA issuers and KYC providers want a complete service, not an SDK. Atka §70: "ironically, onboarding XRP holders into DeFi runs through CEXes" → CEX→DeFi-yield bridge service, not a tool. Pattern: the most monetizable opportunities are AI-native services that internalize operations.

Section 03 · Per-PWG findings

Findings by working group

Each PWG's specifics + a global Atka synthesis.

PWG 1 · Lending

Lending (XLS-66/65)

  • Amendment brick: XLS-66 (native lending primitive) — not yet live (voting Jan 2026)
  • Tension: generic pool (Aave-like) vs vault providers (Morpho-like)
  • Critical gap: no institutional uncollateralized lending (Maple/Goldfinch) — this is where the TradFi margin is made
  • Opportunity: "Compliant Credit Vault" — agent-curator that underwrites institutional borrowers via XLS-70 attestations
PWG 2 · RWA

Real-World Assets

  • Brick: MPT (XLS-33d) + AMM XLS-30 + DID XLS-70
  • Gap: no RWA-spec standard (who holds what, dividend rights, voting, redemption)
  • Tension: Ondo & OpenEden do RWA "off-XRPL then bridge" — XRPL is settlement, not native issuance
  • Opportunity: "RWA Issuance Studio" for European SMEs/asset managers — MiFID + MPT tokenization + cap table in 1 service
PWG 3 · Stablecoins

Stablecoins

  • Brick: RLUSD live, EURC pending, MiCA-compliant
  • Gap: no native EUR stablecoin on XRPL; no programmable stablecoin (revenue-share, programmable yield)
  • Opportunity: "Programmable RLUSD treasury" — DAO/corporate treasury layer that automates RLUSD allocation → vault yields
PWG 4 · Identity

Identity & Credentials

  • Brick: XLS-70 (DID), Credentials (live testnet)
  • Huge gap: no product-market-fit on credential usage — not a single XRPL DeFi protocol in production consumes an XLS-70 attestation
  • Opportunity: "KYC-as-a-Credential" service — a KYC issuer (alternative to Sumsub/Onfido) that delivers a reusable XRPL credential. Massive captive market.
Section 04 · Atka — DeFi global view

The 9 Atka recommendations in synthesis

The XRPL DeFi Report (89 slides, Jan 2026) proposes a North Star and 9 growth recommendations. Condensed synthesis:

Proposed North Star: "Become the leading institutional & compliant DeFi chain"

1. Position institutional/compliant · 2. Unified XRPL+EVM messaging · 3. Match sub-segments to the XRP holder persona (yield-seeking, long-term)

4. EVM Sidechain Labs (dedicated EVM BD team) · 5. Push specific concepts on EVM (Babylon-fork for XRP, Morpho-fork, Prediction × Synthetics)

6. Partner with cross-chain landmarks (Morpho, Babylon, Pendle, Securitize) · 7. EVM Sidechain funding alleys (extend XRPL Grants/Aquarium/Ripple Investments to EVM)

8. Double down on institution-centric XRPL native (RLUSD, RWA, Lending) · 9. GTM via CEX (CeDeFi mandatory — 60% of XRP is on CEXes)
